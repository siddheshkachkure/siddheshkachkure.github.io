10 / HOW I WORK

Engineering discipline
under pressure.

The goal is not just to restore service. It is to understand the failure domain, communicate clearly and prevent recurrence.

01

Evidence before assumptions

Correlate application metrics, node state, network counters, client behavior and storage telemetry before declaring a bottleneck.

02

Contain, then resolve

Stabilize the workload, document impact and work with OEM/internal teams toward a tested permanent fix.

03

Operate the lifecycle

Capacity, maintenance, upgrades, observability, incident/change/problem management and customer communication all matter.

04

Validate before production

Use POCs, feature validation, workload-shaped benchmarks and acceptance criteria to reduce deployment risk.
